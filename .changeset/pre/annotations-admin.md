Annotations: `admin.annotations` in `./admin` with `list`, `create`, `update` and `remove` over `/v2/projects/:project/annotations`. Dates are a `Date`, a calendar date or an ISO 8601 timestamp; a malformed date, a non-http(s) URL, an unknown `kind` or an empty update is a type error.
